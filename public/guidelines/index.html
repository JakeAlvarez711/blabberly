Community Guidelines

Effective: September 7, 2026 · Version 2 · Replaces the version dated August 24, 2026

In short: Post real food from places you actually went, be decent to people, and keep other people's private lives out of it. If you see something that breaks these rules, report it and we will look.

Blabberly is a place to share what you ate, where, and whether it was worth it. These guidelines keep it useful and safe. They are part of our Terms of Service. Breaking them can get Content removed or your account suspended or closed.

1. Keep it real

  • Post places you actually visited and food you actually tried. Your photos, videos, and captions should be yours and from that visit.
  • Rate honestly. A rating is your real opinion. Do not rate a place you have not been to, do not inflate or tank a rating to help or hurt a business, and do not rate your own restaurant.
  • No fake accounts, no bots, no coordinated campaigns. One person, one account.
  • Disclose relationships. If you work at, own, or are related to someone at a restaurant, say so in the post. If a business gave you free food, a discount, or money for a post, say so clearly in the caption. Undisclosed paid posts and reviews are removed, and businesses that arrange them can lose their listing privileges.
  • Businesses: claim your place when that feature is available, and do not pose as a customer. You may not ask users to post or rate in exchange for anything without the user disclosing it.

2. Be decent

  • No harassment or bullying. No threats, no targeting people with repeated unwanted contact, no encouraging others to pile on.
  • No hate. Content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, age, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, or immigration status is removed.
  • Restaurant staff and owners are people. Critique the food and the experience. Do not name, shame, or attack individual employees.
  • Disagree about food, not about each other. A bad review is fine. A personal attack is not.

3. Respect privacy

  • Do not post other people's private information: phone numbers, addresses, where someone lives or works, license plates, or anything that identifies a person who did not agree to be in your post.
  • Do not share anyone's location or track people. Live location on a route is for the people on that route while it is happening, nothing else.
  • Ask before you post people. Do not post recognizable photos or videos of other diners, staff, or children without permission. Only tag people who are okay with being tagged.
  • Messages are private to the conversation. Do not screenshot and post private messages to embarrass someone.

4. Keep it appropriate

  • No sexual content or nudity. Blabberly is open to people 13 and up.
  • No graphic violence, gore, or content that glorifies self-harm.
  • Alcohol: posting about a bar or a cocktail is fine. Content that promotes underage drinking, dangerous drinking, or driving after drinking is not.
  • No illegal drugs and no Content that promotes illegal activity.
  • No spam. No repetitive posts, unrelated promotions, link dumps, or off-topic content. Blabberly is about food, drinks, and places.
  • Respect copyright. Post what you made or have permission to share. Do not upload other people's photos, videos, or music. Music from our sound library is licensed for use in Blabberly videos only.

5. Safety

  • Meetups and plans happen between people. Use your judgment, especially with people you only know from the App, and meet in public places.
  • Never use the App while driving. Plan routes before you leave, and plan a ride if you will be drinking.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first, then report to us.

6. Reporting and what happens next

  • How to report. Long-press a post, comment, or message and choose Report, or open a profile and choose Report or Block. You can also email support@blabberly.com with a link or screenshot.
  • What we do. Every report is reviewed by a person at Blabberly. Images and video frames in new posts are also scanned automatically for adult, violent, or racy content, and anything flagged goes to a person for review. Nothing is removed automatically. We aim to review reports within a day.
  • Actions we may take: remove the Content, warn the account, restrict features such as messaging or posting, suspend the account temporarily, or close it permanently. Severe or repeated violations, and anything involving minors, illegal content, or credible threats, lead to immediate removal. We report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and cooperate with law enforcement as the law requires.
  • Blocking. Block anyone you do not want to hear from. A blocked person cannot message you, follow you, or see your profile from their account.
  • Appeals. If you think we got it wrong, email support@blabberly.com within 30 days and tell us why. We will look again and reply.

7. Questions

Email support@blabberly.com. Blabberly is small and in beta; a real person reads every message.
