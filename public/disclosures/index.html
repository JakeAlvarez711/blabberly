Disclosures

Effective: September 7, 2026 · Version 1

In short: Some of the text in Blabberly is written by AI, some place details come from Google and from restaurants' own websites, the app is in beta, and nothing in it is paid placement today. This page says exactly which is which and how to tell us when something is wrong.

1. AI-generated text

We use Anthropic's Claude models to generate some of the text you see about places, events, and menus. The App does not yet label these individually, so this page is the disclosure.

What is AI-generated

  • Place descriptions. The short "about" description on a place page is written by AI unless the business wrote its own or we did. It is generated from the place's name, category, tags, price level, Google's editorial summary, and up to three short excerpts of recent public Blabberly post captions about that place. Captions are used without the author's name or account.
  • Tags and search terms. Cuisine tags, dietary and amenity tags (for example "dog-friendly" or "gluten-free options"), "vibe" tags, and the hidden search terms that help you find places by typing things like "rooftop" or "first date" are generated by AI from place information and, for public routes, from the route's title, description, and stop notes.
  • Events and specials. Some recurring events and specials in the Events section (happy hours, trivia nights, taco Tuesdays) were suggested by AI from restaurant information and then checked by us. Details can still be wrong or out of date.
  • Menus. Menus are collected from restaurants' own websites by our software, and AI is used to turn the text or PDF into a structured list of items and prices. Menus show when they were last updated and can be out of date. Prices are not guaranteed.

What is not AI-generated

  • Anything a user posted: photos, videos, captions, comments, ratings, and rating notes.
  • Hours, addresses, and photos from Google or from the business.
  • Descriptions a business wrote itself.

What goes into the AI

Place information (name, address, category, tags, hours, Google's editorial summary, and in older runs Google review text), menu text from restaurants' own websites, short excerpts of public post captions, and public route titles and notes. No account information, no private messages, no location data, and no private content is ever sent. This runs as a batch job by Blabberly staff on our own computers, not from your phone.

If something is wrong

Email support@blabberly.com with the place name and what is wrong. We correct or remove inaccurate descriptions, tags, events, and menu items. Businesses can ask us to replace an AI description with their own.

2. Location

The App uses your location only while you are using it, and only if you allow it in iOS. It is used to detect which restaurant you are at when you post, show nearby places, center the map, and sort saved places by distance. Your location is sent to our servers only when you choose to share it: in a chat, on a live route with "Share my live location" on, or as an approximate position when you search for a place. We never track location in the background. Posts store the location of the place you tagged, not where you were standing. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

3. Beta status

Blabberly is in beta, distributed through Apple TestFlight to testers in San Diego County. Features may change or break, some features you can see in the App are turned off, and we may occasionally need to reset data. Report bugs from Settings, then About & Support, or email support@blabberly.com. Our Terms of Service describe the beta program.

4. Businesses, partners, and paid placement

  • No paid placement today. No business has paid to appear higher in the feed, on the map, in search, or in Explore, and no business has paid for a post or a rating.
  • When we launch partner tools, businesses will be able to claim their listing, manage their menu, and pay to be featured. Featured placements will carry a visible "Featured" label wherever they appear, so you can always tell paid placement from organic results. Ratings and reviews will never be for sale.
  • Businesses do not receive information about you. When partner tools launch, they will see counts, such as how many people viewed their menu, not who you are.
  • Undisclosed sponsored posts are against our Community Guidelines. If a business gave a user free food or money for a post, the post must say so.

5. Where place information comes from

Place details come from several sources and can disagree with each other or with reality:

  • Google Maps Platform (Places API): names, addresses, hours, price levels, and photos for many places. Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply to that data.
  • Restaurants' own websites: menus and, where offered, descriptions.
  • Blabberly research and automated tools: tags, descriptions, and events (section 1).
  • Users: posts, ratings, dish names, and place suggestions.

Hours, prices, and availability change. Confirm with the business before you go. Listing a business does not mean we are affiliated with it or endorse it.

6. Reporting a security problem

If you find a security vulnerability in the App or the Site, email support@blabberly.com. Our security contact is also published at blabberly.com/.well-known/security.txt. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix the problem before sharing it publicly.
